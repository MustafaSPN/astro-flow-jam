## Description (4000)
Slide colorful blocks out of the board and watch them build a beautiful pixel picture!

Astro Flow Jam is a relaxing yet clever block puzzle set in space. Every block you free flies up and fills in the picture above the board. Clear the whole board to reveal the image and add it to your collection.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag a block toward the gate with the same color to send it out
• Plan your moves: blocks can block each other's way
• Use the holding slots wisely – when they fill up, the level is lost
• Beat the clock and finish the picture

FEATURES
• Over 100 levels that get trickier as you go, with more on the way
• Special blocks like frozen blocks that thaw as you play
• Handy boosters for tough moments
• A growing collection of cute space pictures to unlock
• Short levels, perfect for quick breaks
• Smooth 3D blocks and satisfying animations

Free to play with optional in-app purchases. Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase.
